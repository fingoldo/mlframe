## Checked, no finding

- `greedy_backward_elimination.py:56-80`: the trace score is monotone (each accepted step has `best_score > current_score + tol`), so `max(score_after)` equals the kept-set score when the trace is non-empty. The reported `selection_score` in `_arms_wrappers.py:176` is therefore correct; only the empty-trace None (11-F17) is an issue.
- `_arms.py:146-169` (`FitCounter`): the own-vs-inherited `fit` restore logic is correct, and reentrancy across the listed estimator classes is clean because none of them subclasses another listed class.
- `stat_study/core.py:204-210` (`ols_t`): the shift `t = b2*su/b3 - mu` is the correct solution of `c/d` for the (u+t)*v interaction under the standardised regressors. The winsorised path and the clamp are consistent.
- `offset_kernels.py:168-205` (variant b rank-to-bin mapping): the marked-bin gather offsets (`off[b] + rank - cum_before[b]`) address the correct order statistic. Variant a and b agree on the edge arithmetic (`_rank_positions` vs `_edges_partition`).
- `_arm_result.py:__post_init__`: the support dtype/length, score finiteness, `score_kind` and `ranked_prefix` contracts are all enforced and raise rather than degrade.
- `common/downstream.py:61-71`: models are fit only on the train split, and R^2 is reference-only as documented, so no leakage in the metric path.
- `wiring.py:24` (REPO = parents[5]) resolves to the repository root, and `plan` counts anchors on CRLF-normalised text, so the dry run and `apply` agree on counts.
