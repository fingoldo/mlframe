- `bench_logical_constraints.py:52-53` warm-up: `_run(50, ...)` warms the single-thread njit kernel because `n < _PARALLEL_THRESHOLD=20_000` (`logical_constraints.py:35`), and `_run(30_000, ...)` warms the parallel variant. The comment is accurate.
- `bench_entity_prediction_collapse.py:38`: the weighted quantile numba kernel `_weighted_quantile_by_group_numba` (`entity_prediction_collapse.py:20-21`) is warmed before timing. The unweighted quantile and mean paths are pandas-only (`entity_prediction_collapse.py:84-95`), so no JIT cost is hidden in those timings.
