## Checked, no finding

- `select_features_fdr` (`_knockoffs.py:147-200`): the knockoff+ threshold `(1 + #{W <= -t}) / max(1, #{W >= t}) <= q` is implemented with correct counts (searchsorted with `side="left"` on sorted magnitudes), and the candidate set is the sorted positive `|W|`. Matches Barber-Candes 2015.
- Stability PFER bound (`_stability_select.py:379-384`): `q^2 / ((2*pi - 1) * p)`, with the `pi > 0.5` guard. Formula and guard are correct.
- Bootstrap OOB evaluation (`_stability_select.py:121-136`): importances are measured on out-of-bag rows, with an explicit fallback when nothing is held out. Consistent with its docstring.
- `aggregate_linear` (`_helpers_importance_agg.py:204-235`): the vectorised sign-agreement and weighted-mean logic checked out for zero rows, all-NaN rows and single-run features.
- `_rank_sort_key` and the F7 lexicographic tie-break (`_helpers_importance.py:601-605, 672-674`): NaN and -inf rank last, ties are deterministic.
- `_impute_ragged_fi_table` `worst` branch (`_helpers_importance.py:577-595`): the epsilon scaling and all-NaN column fallback are correct for the legacy path.
- Fast default scorer latch (`_helpers_importance.py:164-190`): the closed form is only latched after an exact baseline match, and NaN-vs-NaN is handled.
- Fold-state locking (`_fit_fold.py:47, 256-272, 404-407`): the shared containers are only mutated under the lock.
