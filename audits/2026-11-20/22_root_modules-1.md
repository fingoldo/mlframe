## Checked, no finding

- calibration/_independence_check.py: the closed-form leave-one-out covariance and variance algebra (lines 46-72) matches a direct computation. The only defect is the degenerate-member handling (22-F1).
- data_valuation/_knn_shapley.py:33-46: the KNN-Shapley recursion, including the `min(k, i) / i` factor and the reverse-order fill, matches Jia et al. (VLDB 2019).

- calibration/quality.py:619-641: the Anderson-Darling kernel matches A^2 = -n - (1/n) sum (2i-1)[ln F(x_i) + ln(1 - F(x_{n+1-i}))].
- evaluation/_bootstrap_fused_binary_bundle.py:106-110: the AUC trapezoid over distinct groups divided by 2PN is correct for both tie-free and grouped counting.
- core/proportion_stats.py:60-66: the Wilson score interval formula is correct.
- competition/panel_target_persistence.py:251-267: lag and lead within group are correct because same-group is checked on sorted adjacency.
- core/ewma.py: the NaN-ageing rule matches pandas `ignore_na=False`.
- _lightgbm_thread_safety.py and _numba_parallel_guard.py: RLock re-entrancy is correct for nested guards.
