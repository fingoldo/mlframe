| STABILITY_MISC-1 | P1 | bug (silent wrong selection) | `group_aware.py:403-406` | The polars→pandas bridge builds `pd.DataFrame(X.to_numpy(), columns=...)`. For a polars frame with ANY non-numeric column, `pl.DataFrame.to_numpy()` returns an **object** 2-D array, and `pd.DataFrame(object_2d)` keeps **every** column object-dtype — including the genuinely numeric ones. Downstream `_numeric_codes_frame` (`:103`) then sees `is_numeric_dtype==False` for those numeric columns and `pd.factorize`s them (`:107`), which assigns **appearance-order** integer codes (`sort=False` default), destroying the numeric ordering. So the correlation / SU redundancy matrix, the clustering, and the medoids are computed on order-of-appearance codes instead of real values → a silently different (wrong) `cluster_assignments_`/`support_` vs the identical data passed as a pandas frame. | `X = pl.DataFrame({"a":[3.,1.,2.,4.], "b":[6.,2.,4.,8.], "c":["x","y","z","w"]})` — pandas fit clusters a,b together (corr≈1); the polars path factorizes a→[0,1,2,3], b→[0,1,2,3] by appearance order, so corr(a,b) no longer reflects the real relationship and the medoid/cluster result diverges from the pandas fit. Confirmed `pd.DataFrame(np.array([[1.,'a'],...],dtype=object))` keeps the numeric column object-dtype. |
