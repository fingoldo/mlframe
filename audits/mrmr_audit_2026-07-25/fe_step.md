# FE_STEP — FE-step orchestration / budget / schedule / vote — audit (2026-07-25)

Scope: the `_mrmr_fe_step/` subpackage (`__init__.py`, `_helpers.py`, `_step_core.py`, `_step_pairmi.py`,
`_step_pairs_rank.py`, `_step_pool.py`, `_step_score.py`) plus the FE budget/schedule/vote siblings
(`_mrmr_fe_step_helpers.py`, `_fe_rung_schedule.py`, `_fe_auto_escalation.py`, `_fe_deadline.py`,
`_fe_family_budget.py`, `_fe_family_timing.py`, `_fe_rejection_ledger.py`, `_fe_stability_vote.py`,
`_fe_sufficient_summary.py`, `_fe_subsample.py`). This is the FE-pair pipeline orchestrator: operand-pool
build, pair-MI/maxT floor, ranking, per-candidate CMI-redundancy + prune + discretise/materialise +
auto-escalation + additive-fusion + cross-fold stability vote, and the compute-budget/deadline/rejection-
ledger scaffolding around it. Pure in-process numeric orchestration — no DB/HTTP/UI/eval/exec surface
(security checklist N/A). Every prior-audit FE_STEP finding was re-checked against current source; the
verified-FIXED ones (see the Non-findings section) are not re-reported.
