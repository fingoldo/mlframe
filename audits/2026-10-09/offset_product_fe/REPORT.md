# Offset-product feature engineering: what the preset cannot express, and how to add it, 2026-10-09

Scope: the red test `tests/feature_selection/mrmr/biz_val/test_biz_value_mrmr_gate_vs_elementary.py::test_case2_warped_cd_interaction_still_captured_with_gate_on`, the measurements behind it, and two independent reviews (statistics, integration/cost) of the proposed fix. Status values: OPEN, IN PROGRESS, RESOLVED, NOT A DEFECT, FUTURE.

## The case

`y = 0.2*a**2/b + f/5 + log(2c)*sin(d/3)`, all columns U(0,1), n = 30000. The `a, b, f` terms are independent of `(c, d)`. The `(c, d)` part is a product `f(c)*g(d)` with `f = ln 2 + ln c` and `g = sin(d/3)` (nearly linear on (0, 1)).

The test fails, and failed before the changes of 2026-10-09 (same numbers in the older worktree `wt_head`). MRMR selects `['c', 'mul(sqr(a),reciproc(b))', 'gate_mask__c__d__t0.24814', 'esc_poly_laguerre_mul(b,d)']`; the `(c, d)` pair is rejected by `engineered_mi_prevalence` (best engineered MI / pair joint MI = 0.816 < 0.9) and only the gate mask survives, with MI 0.2004 < raw `c` 0.2454.

## Decision record

Recommended design (both reviews agree on the stage-level shape): a separate stage (about 350-450 LOC, new module, new recipe kind `offset_product` with `t` in `extra`), not an insertion into the pair phase (about 600 LOC across about 10 files; `_pairs_core.py` already 1034 LOC and `_usability_njit_pool.py` 945). Candidates `mul(u - t, v)` for the top-K product forms of the top pairs by joint MI, with `t` chosen without a search where possible (zero-crossing, median) or by a small grid on a <= 20k subsample, winners re-scored at full n; acceptance by the whole-grid permutation null (max over K x G) and a margin of the null q95 gain; joint-MI ceiling with Miller-Madow on both sides. Operators must be njit / prange / CUDA (user requirement 2026-10-09). Fixed shifted unaries (`log1p`, `log(2x)`) are a cheap optional second step; fixing the 0.9 gate for small n is a separate item.

## Review 1: statistics (agent a-stat), verbatim summary

Verdict: do not add the grid as a search; the shift is the right ingredient (0.2826 -> 0.3266 on the case; passes the 0.9 x joint ceiling at 0.950) but selection bias is small, so a closed-form or nonparametric version is the honest option; the prevalence gate is not the bug in this case.

Selection bias: grid parametrised as `(u+t)*v` with `t = -quantile_q(u)`, q in 0.1..0.9. Pure-noise gain of the grid max about 0.001 MI at n = 10k, 10 bins (0.0007-0.0010 at G = 5-17; 0.0008 Miller-Madow; 0.002 at 20 bins). Flawed null (permute y, rescore only the selected t): false-accept 0.23-0.60 at G >= 5. Correct null (rerun the whole grid): 0.00-0.17 on 30 replicates (noisy). Family level on noise: adding the ~1150-candidate shift family to the 512 preset candidates raises the best MI by 0.0009 (q95 0.0017) at n = 5k, 10 bins; 0.0015 (q95 0.0041) at 20 bins; 0.0001 (q95 0.0003) at n = 30k. When the base form is already right the gain is 0.0009 (q95 0.0025) at 5k, 0.0001 (q95 0.0004) at 30k. The preset search is the larger bias (n = 5k noise best MI 0.0115 vs 0.004 for a single candidate). Held-out selection (choose on half A, score on half B) costs almost nothing on the case (0.2313 -> 0.2345), `(x-.5)*z` 0.3151 -> 0.3098, `log(x+.3)*z` 0.2933 -> 0.2926; XOR 0.179 -> 0.163.

Generality (n = 30000, SNR 1, 10 bins, minimal preset):

| target | truth | preset best | + shift grid | joint | grid passes 0.9 x joint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| log(2x) sin(z/3) | .231 | .210 | .231 | .253 | yes (.914) |
| (x-.5) z | .312 | .264 | .312 | .336 | yes |
| log(x+.3) z | .292 | .270 | .292 | .314 | yes |
| (e^x-1.8) z | .314 | .249 | .313 | .337 | yes |
| sqrt(x+.1) sin(3z) | .335 | .220 | .267 | .338 | no (.79) |
| z/(x+.1) | .237 | .234 | .232 | .261 | no (input shift gives .237, passes at .907) |
| log(x) sin(z), no shift needed | .239 | .239 | .239 | .261 | yes (preset passes) |
| x^2 + z, additive | .325 | .325 | no gain | .342 | n/a |
| XOR sign | .329 | .169 | .172 | .336 | no (median-centering gives .322) |

Off-median crossings:

| target | preset best | shift grid | median-centered | zero-crossing | ALS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (x-.25) z | .3025 | .3080 | .2851 | .3089 | .2900 |
| (x-.75) z | .2910 | .3180 | .2885 | .3184 | .3018 |
| log(3x) sin(z/3) | .2096 | .2373 | .2306 | .2385 | .2378 |
| log(1.3x) sin(z) | .2290 | .2334 | .2253 | .2343 | .2174 |

Parametrisation: median-centering loses 0.02-0.03 off the median. The data-driven zero-crossing (8 quantile bins of u, per-bin sign of corr(v, y), t at the strongest flip) has no search and matches the grid on 7 of 8 shift-type targets; misses were `sqrt(x+.1) sin(3z)` and XOR. Input shift `log(x+k)` is worse than the output shift on most targets but wins on `z/(x+.1)`. A 2-parameter affine gave no consistent gain (0.2278 vs 0.2312). On the real case: shift grid .3266, median-centered .3266, zero-crossing .3159, input shift .3166.

Prevalence ceiling: ratio of the true 1-D form MI to the joint 2-D MI is 0.635 / 0.804 / 0.886 / 0.953 / 0.976 at n = 2k / 5k / 10k / 30k / 100k with 10 raw bins; with Miller-Madow 1.01 / .998 / .994 / .991 / .988; with 20 raw bins 0.346 / .524 / .682 / .863 / .953. Fix: Miller-Madow on both sides or subtract the permutation-null mean.

Nonparametric rank-1 (16 quantile bins per operand, alternating binned means): use rank(y) as the target; on raw y the heavy-tailed `0.2*a^2/b` term ruins it (MI 0.086, OOS 0.092). On rank(y), real case: in-sample 0.3206, 5-fold OOS 0.3251 (not repeated across seeds). Matches the truth on multiplicative targets (`(x-.5)z` .310, `(e^x-1.8)z` .313, XOR .320, `log(3x)sin(z/3)` .238); fails on additive targets (.141 vs .325) and `z/(x+.1)` (.206). Its output is a piecewise-linear lookup table that needs a storable recipe.

Recommended: three candidates for the top-K product forms (`mul(u - t_ZC, v)`, `mul(u - median(u), v)`, the rank-1 ALS feature on rank(y)); the 9-point q-grid only as optional escalation, with the whole-grid permutation null; Miller-Madow on engineered and joint MI; beat the preset best by the null q95 gain and confirm on a held-out half; keep the later redundancy and CMI gates. Not closed: shift families weak on `sqrt(x+k) sin(z)` and ratio-like targets; one seed per target; minimal preset only; 15-30 replicate nulls; zero-crossing fails on noisy or non-monotone effects; replay and pickling untested.
