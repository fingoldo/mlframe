# 01 Low-hanging follow-ups of the offset-product round (2026-10-10)

Scope: the offset-product pair FE family (`_offset_product_fe.py`, `_offset_product_kernels.py`, `_offset_product_gpu.py`), the FE operator factory
(`src/mlframe/feature_selection/_benchmarks/fe_operator_factory/`) and the GPU launch/copy budget of the MRMR fit. Seven improvements proposed by the owner-approved list
of 2026-10-10 (items L-01 to L-07) plus one finding from profiling the new stage (L-08). The owner asked for all of them to be tracked; none is implemented yet unless the disposition says so.
Method: the stage was profiled with cProfile on a quiet box with the strict-resident switch on (`D:/Temp/agent_brain/prof_stage.py`, to be moved into `_benchmarks/offset_product/`); the other
items come from the statistics study (`audits/2026-10-09/offset_product_fe/FOLLOWUP.md`, S6-S9) and the launch/copy profile of the F2 fit.
Not run: the full F2 fit A/B with and without the family on a quiet box (the box was busy with test runs and an agent). Measured instead: the `maximal`-preset canonical fit takes 1283 s without the family and 1153 s with it (`test_canonical_across_presets[maximal]` carries a 1500 s timeout of its own), so the 2400 s timeout seen in the pinned-test run was that test's inherent length under load, not the family.

| L-01 | `_offset_product_fe.py` (pair loop in `hybrid_offset_product_fe`) | P3 | The scan scores every pair of the top 6 columns (15 pairs x 49 unary pairs = 735 tasks). The statistics study (S9, `stat_study/exp11_prefilter.py`) found a window - Miller-Madow ratio of the preset-best MI to the pair's joint MI in [0.3, 0.9) and joint MI above 0.02 - that kept the planted pair in 5 of 5 planted datasets at n = 5000 and 30000 and kept 1 to 5.7 of 10 pairs (none on pure noise). | The scan cost grows with the number of pairs (O(p^2)); with 6 columns it is 0.12 s on the device at 100k rows but 1-2 s on the CPU, and the pool size is a parameter users may raise. | Pre-filter pairs by the window before the scan; keep the unfiltered scan behind a switch for the recall test. | RESOLVED - the study's window needs the preset-best MI, which costs as much as the scan itself, so the filter uses raw data only: a pair is scanned if its joint 10 x 10 Miller-Madow MI exceeds the larger marginal MI by 0.02 nats (`synergy_pairs`, `SYNERGY_MIN`); on the 300 pairs of the study (25 planted) recall is 1.00 (smallest planted headroom 0.041) and 31% of the other pairs are kept; `min_synergy=None` restores the full scan. Pinned by `test_synergy_prefilter_keeps_the_planted_pair_and_drops_noise_pairs` and `test_prefilter_changes_no_accepted_column`. | L-08 | `_offset_product_fe.py` host work around the scan | P3 | Found while profiling for L-02. Host-side rank of y (`scipy.stats.rankdata`, two calls, 0.38 s at 1M), 41 `np.quantile` calls (0.06 s at 100k, 0.18 s at 1M) and a full-n `encode_y_for_classif_mi` were the largest items. Changed: the full-n rank is computed only when a candidate is accepted, the winsorisation quantiles are one call per column, and the target classes are coded on the scan rows only. Measured effect of the first round: 0.313 -> 0.316 s at 100k (none) and 0.966 -> 0.934 s at 1M (3%). A line-level profile at 1M then showed two full-n `argsort` calls (0.26 s) and 15 `partition` calls on full columns (0.15 s); the ranks of all rows are now a binary search against the sorted scan rows (`_rank_all_rows`) and the winsorisation and output-clip quantiles come from the scan rows: 0.934 -> 0.743 s at 1M, 100k unchanged. | The stage is 0.3-0.9 s of a 5-11 s fit; the remaining host time at 1M has not been attributed. | Move what is left (the unary maps and the refit pass over the full column of each accepted candidate, about 0.15 s, plus the device-scan wait of 0.07 s) into the device-born path of L-02. | PARTIAL |
| L-09 | `_offset_product_fe.py` acceptance rule | P2 | Found at 100k and 1M rows after the pre-filter: the margin 40 / n is 0.0004 nats at 100k scan rows, and the pair (a, d) of the sign-crossing target (no interaction; two additive effects) was accepted with a gain of a fraction of a percent over the baselines. | A column no consumer can use costs a slot of `top_k` and a redundancy check downstream. | Require the held-out gain to be at least `ACCEPT_MIN_RELATIVE_GAIN` = 3% of the best baseline held-out MI as well. | RESOLVED - at 20k, 100k and 1M rows only the (c, d) interaction is accepted, noise and additive targets accept nothing; `test_large_n_accepts_only_the_real_interaction` pins it. |
| L-10 | `_offset_product_fe.py` replay of the `log` unary | P1 | Found while reading `smart_log` for the device path of L-02: the registry's `log` shifts a column by `1e-5 - min(x)` when the minimum is not positive, and the minimum is taken over the array it is given. The first version of the family replayed through the registry, so a transform batch with another minimum (a single row, a subset, a shifted period) was shifted differently from the fit column. `unary_binary` recipes avoid this with a frozen `log_shift_<side>` anchor; the new family did not. | The engineered column silently changed meaning between fit and transform whenever a source column had non-positive values. | Compute the anchor on the whole fit column, use `log(x + anchor)` in the scan, the refit and the replay, and store `log_shift_u` / `log_shift_v` in the recipe. | RESOLVED - `_log_anchor`, `_apply_unary`; `test_log_unary_replays_with_the_frozen_anchor_not_the_batch_minimum` (fails against the registry replay on a strictly positive batch) and `test_fit_stores_the_log_anchor_of_a_column_with_non_positive_values`. |
| L-11 | `_offset_product_fe.py` acceptance (unrelated pairs) | P1 | Found by the whole-MRMR gating check (`_benchmarks/offset_product/gating_check.py`, dataset `sign`, seed 2): the stage accepted `offmul(sqr(a)-0.59,sqrt(d)-1.75)` for the pair (a, d), whose effects are independent and additive. Measured over 20 seeds at n = 15000 and 8 at 100k (`y = 0.2a^2/b + f/5 + ln(2c) sin(d/3)`): the unrelated pair was accepted in 7 of 20 and 4 of 8 runs. Cause: a product with shifts far outside the data range is a weighted sum of its factors, and a weighted sum with better weights than the least-squares baseline (the baseline in the scan) beats every fixed baseline without any interaction; a free-form additive model (32-bin main effects) did not help because its 64 parameters cost more held-out MI than the two-parameter candidate gains. Held-out MI of the candidate over the MI-optimal weighted sum of its own factors: +0.045 to +0.050 nats for the real (c, d) pair against -0.0002 to +0.0098 for (a, d). | An engineered column with no interaction is selected, takes a slot of `top_k` and feeds nested composites in the next FE step. | Three changes, none a hand-set margin over n: (1) the best weighted sum `cos(t) u/sd(u) + sin(t) v/sd(v)` of the winner's own factors (32 directions, chosen on the even rows, scored on the odd rows; `weighted_sum_heldout_mi`) joins the baselines; (2) the gain must exceed `SIGNIFICANCE_Z` = 2 standard errors of the paired MI difference (delta method: spread of the per-row difference of the pointwise MIs over sqrt(n_odd), `_winner_standard_error`), so the bar follows n and the noise; (3) the practical effect, a fraction of the baseline MI, is the constructor knob `fe_offset_product_min_relative_gain` (default 0.05) instead of a buried constant. Rejected on the way (bench-attempt-rejected, recorded in the module docstring): a free-form additive baseline (its 64 parameters lose more held-out MI than the 2-parameter candidate gains), and a surrogate-target null (additive fit plus residuals permuted within prediction bins, whole per-pair procedure on each of 19 draws): it accepted an unrelated pair in 28 of 28 runs and cost 1.3 s per fit because the surrogate removes every interaction, including the genuine a**2/b one that the shift-free baselines already capture, so the baseline-relative gain is not comparable with its null. | RESOLVED - unrelated pairs accepted: 0 in 28 runs of the sign-crossing target (15k x 20, 60k x 8), in 20 runs of a purely additive nonlinear target and in 20 pure-noise runs; the real pair kept in 28 of 28 (10 of 20 at half amplitude); 0.26 s per fit. Pinned by `test_independent_additive_effects_are_not_credited_as_an_interaction`, `test_weighted_sum_baseline_separates_an_additive_target_from_an_interaction`, `test_standard_error_of_the_gain_shrinks_with_the_square_root_of_n` and the teeth test (under the pre-fix rule an unrelated pair is accepted on these seeds). |
