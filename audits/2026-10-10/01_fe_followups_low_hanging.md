# 01 Low-hanging follow-ups of the offset-product round (2026-10-10)

Scope: the offset-product pair FE family (`_offset_product_fe.py`, `_offset_product_kernels.py`, `_offset_product_gpu.py`), the FE operator factory
(`src/mlframe/feature_selection/_benchmarks/fe_operator_factory/`) and the GPU launch/copy budget of the MRMR fit. Seven improvements proposed by the owner-approved list
of 2026-10-10 (items L-01 to L-07) plus one finding from profiling the new stage (L-08). The owner asked for all of them to be tracked; none is implemented yet unless the disposition says so.
Method: the stage was profiled with cProfile on a quiet box with the strict-resident switch on (`D:/Temp/agent_brain/prof_stage.py`, to be moved into `_benchmarks/offset_product/`); the other
items come from the statistics study (`audits/2026-10-09/offset_product_fe/FOLLOWUP.md`, S6-S9) and the launch/copy profile of the F2 fit.
Not run: the full F2 fit A/B with and without the family on a quiet box (the box was busy with test runs and an agent). Measured instead: the `maximal`-preset canonical fit takes 1283 s without the family and 1153 s with it (`test_canonical_across_presets[maximal]` carries a 1500 s timeout of its own), so the 2400 s timeout seen in the pinned-test run was that test's inherent length under load, not the family.

## Findings
| ID | file:line | sev | evidence | impact | proposed fix | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L-01 | `_offset_product_fe.py` (pair loop in `hybrid_offset_product_fe`) | P3 | The scan scores every pair of the top 6 columns (15 pairs x 49 unary pairs = 735 tasks). The statistics study (S9, `stat_study/exp11_prefilter.py`) found a window - Miller-Madow ratio of the preset-best MI to the pair's joint MI in [0.3, 0.9) and joint MI above 0.02 - that kept the planted pair in 5 of 5 planted datasets at n = 5000 and 30000 and kept 1 to 5.7 of 10 pairs (none on pure noise). | The scan cost grows with the number of pairs (O(p^2)); with 6 columns it is 0.12 s on the device at 100k rows but 1-2 s on the CPU, and the pool size is a parameter users may raise. | Pre-filter pairs by the window before the scan; keep the unfiltered scan behind a switch for the recall test. | RESOLVED - the study's window needs the preset-best MI, which costs as much as the scan itself, so the filter uses raw data only: a pair is scanned if its joint 10 x 10 Miller-Madow MI exceeds the larger marginal MI by 0.02 nats (`synergy_pairs`, `SYNERGY_MIN`); on the 300 pairs of the study (25 planted) recall is 1.00 (smallest planted headroom 0.041) and 31% of the other pairs are kept; `min_synergy=None` restores the full scan. Pinned by `test_synergy_prefilter_keeps_the_planted_pair_and_drops_noise_pairs` and `test_prefilter_changes_no_accepted_column`. Stage time on the device is unchanged (0.316 -> 0.298 s at 100k: the scan is only 0.12 s there); the gain is on the CPU path and with larger pools. |
| L-02 | `_offset_product_fe.py:_scan_inputs`, `_offset_product_gpu.py:scan_offset_products_gpu` | P3 | Unary outputs of the pooled columns are built on the host and uploaded (6 x 7 x 100k doubles = 34 MB at the default scan size, 100 MB at 300k). Profile of the whole stage with the device scan: 0.316 s at 100k rows and 0.934 s at 1M, of which the device scan is 0.12 s at 100k. | In strict-resident mode the device-born rule says no intermediate should cross the bus when the columns are already resident. | Build the unary outputs, the winsorisation bounds and the rank-scaled target on the device from the resident columns (`resident_operand`); keep the host path for host frames. | RESOLVED - `scan_offset_products_device` (`_offset_product_gpu.py`) uploads only the scan rows of each pooled column (4.8 MB instead of the 34 MB block), makes the unary outputs, non-finite fills and winsorisation bounds on the device and never copies the block back; unaries without a device form (anything outside `DEVICE_UNARIES`) return None and take the host path. Stage time in strict-resident mode on a quieter box: 0.306 -> 0.194 s at 100k and 0.743 -> 0.442 s at 1M. Pinned by `test_device_built_inputs_match_host_built_inputs` (clips to 1e-9, MI tables to 1e-9 at the 99th percentile, a column with non-positive values included) and `test_device_inputs_decline_a_unary_without_a_device_form`. |
| L-03 | `_offset_product_fe.py:_scan` | P3 | The scan runs on the device whenever `fe_gpu_strict_resident_enabled()` is true, otherwise on the CPU. `scan_bench.py` (busy box) measured the device 5.3x faster at 20k rows, 10.5x at 100k and 10.2x at 300k, so the break-even depends on the host. | A fixed switch is the hard-coded threshold the project rules forbid, and a CUDA host that is not in strict mode never uses the faster path. | Dispatch CPU or device through the kernel tuning cache (`KernelTuningCache` entry for the scan keyed by scan rows and task count) with a sweep registered for `mlframe-tune-kernels ensure`. | PARTIAL - `lookup_offset_scan_backend` (dispatch.py) reads the `offset_product_scan` regions and falls back to the device scan in strict-resident mode, the njit scan otherwise; the sweep `_run_sweep_offset_product_scan` (`_auto_tune_sweeps_c.py`) is registered and found by `ensure --check`; remains: run `mlframe-tune-kernels ensure` on a quiet box so the verdict is measured, and a test of the lookup. |
| L-04 | `_offset_product_kernels.py`, all njit kernels with `cache=True` | P3 | The first call of `hybrid_offset_product_fe` on a cold cache took 10.4 s (numba compilation of the scan kernel); the warm call 0.28 s. | The first fit of a new install or after a source edit pays 10 s inside the user's fit. | Warm the offset-product kernels in `mlframe-tune-kernels ensure` (and in the pre-push ensure hook) the same way the other njit pools are warmed. | PARTIAL - the scan sweep compiles the CPU kernel as a side effect and `offset_product_njit_warmup` (CPU-only hosts) does only that; both are registered; remains: run them and confirm that a cold first fit no longer pays the 10 s. |
| L-05 | `tests/training/_fuzz_combo/axes.py`, `builders.py`, `combo.py`, `enumerator.py` | P3 | Every other family flag has an axis (for example `mrmr_fe_mahalanobis_density_enable_cfg`); `fe_offset_product_enable` has none, and it is on by default. | The fuzz-combination suite never turns the new family off, so a failure that only appears with it on (or off) is not explored. | Add `mrmr_fe_offset_product_enable_cfg: (False, True)` through the four files and the axes test. | RESOLVED - axis (True, False) in axes.py, combo field with canonical collapse to True outside use_mrmr_fs, builder kwarg and enumerator default; `test_offset_product_enable_axis_present_wired_and_gated` and the 18 neighbouring axis tests pass with `--run-fuzz`. |
| L-06 | engineered-feature scale for linear consumers (all families); `_offset_product_fe.py` clips its output at its 0.1% / 99.9% quantiles | P2 | A smoke run of the factory (`downstream.py`) showed one unscaled engineered feature making a ridge model 39x worse in MAE than the raw columns; the statistics study's ridge means were dragged down by single heavy-tailed picks (R^2 of -1.6 and -1.36). | A heavy-tailed engineered column that MI-based selection keeps can ruin a linear model downstream; only the new family clips. | Audit which families emit unbounded columns (ratios, reciprocals, exp, deep composites), measure ridge MAE/RMSE with and without a replay-time clip or rank scaling on the F2-like fixtures, and decide per family. | TODO |
| L-07 | float64 -> float64 copies in the GPU fit | P3 | The launch/copy profile of the F2 fit at 1M rows shows about 1590 device copies of float64 into float64 costing about 80 ms; the default candidate dtype is float32 (`relaxed_candidate_dtype`), yet the owner remembers some FE families left on float64 and that count is high even for those; which families and which call sites is NOT yet established (to be read off the nvprof auto-tag map). | Copies of an array into its own dtype are pure waste; 1590 launches also add to the 13.4k launch count that bounds the fit. | Find the call sites from the nvprof auto-tag map (`_benchmarks/profiling/nvprof_autotag.py`), use `astype(..., copy=False)` / `asarray` where the dtype already matches, and decide per family whether float32 production is safe (selection parity test). | TODO |
| L-08 | `_offset_product_fe.py` host work around the scan | P3 | Found while profiling for L-02. Host-side rank of y (`scipy.stats.rankdata`, two calls, 0.38 s at 1M), 41 `np.quantile` calls (0.06 s at 100k, 0.18 s at 1M) and a full-n `encode_y_for_classif_mi` were the largest items. Changed: the full-n rank is computed only when a candidate is accepted, the winsorisation quantiles are one call per column, and the target classes are coded on the scan rows only. Measured effect of the first round: 0.313 -> 0.316 s at 100k (none) and 0.966 -> 0.934 s at 1M (3%). A line-level profile at 1M then showed two full-n `argsort` calls (0.26 s) and 15 `partition` calls on full columns (0.15 s); the ranks of all rows are now a binary search against the sorted scan rows (`_rank_all_rows`) and the winsorisation and output-clip quantiles come from the scan rows: 0.934 -> 0.743 s at 1M, 100k unchanged. | The stage is 0.3-0.9 s of a 5-11 s fit; the remaining host time at 1M has not been attributed. | Move what is left (the unary maps and the refit pass over the full column of each accepted candidate, about 0.15 s, plus the device-scan wait of 0.07 s) into the device-born path of L-02. | PARTIAL |
| L-09 | `_offset_product_fe.py` acceptance rule | P2 | Found at 100k and 1M rows after the pre-filter: the margin 40 / n is 0.0004 nats at 100k scan rows, and the pair (a, d) of the sign-crossing target (no interaction; two additive effects) was accepted with a gain of a fraction of a percent over the baselines. | A column no consumer can use costs a slot of `top_k` and a redundancy check downstream. | Require the held-out gain to be at least `ACCEPT_MIN_RELATIVE_GAIN` = 3% of the best baseline held-out MI as well. | RESOLVED - at 20k, 100k and 1M rows only the (c, d) interaction is accepted, noise and additive targets accept nothing; `test_large_n_accepts_only_the_real_interaction` pins it. |
| L-10 | `_offset_product_fe.py` replay of the `log` unary | P1 | Found while reading `smart_log` for the device path of L-02: the registry's `log` shifts a column by `1e-5 - min(x)` when the minimum is not positive, and the minimum is taken over the array it is given. The first version of the family replayed through the registry, so a transform batch with another minimum (a single row, a subset, a shifted period) was shifted differently from the fit column. `unary_binary` recipes avoid this with a frozen `log_shift_<side>` anchor; the new family did not. | The engineered column silently changed meaning between fit and transform whenever a source column had non-positive values. | Compute the anchor on the whole fit column, use `log(x + anchor)` in the scan, the refit and the replay, and store `log_shift_u` / `log_shift_v` in the recipe. | RESOLVED - `_log_anchor`, `_apply_unary`; `test_log_unary_replays_with_the_frozen_anchor_not_the_batch_minimum` (fails against the registry replay on a strictly positive batch) and `test_fit_stores_the_log_anchor_of_a_column_with_non_positive_values`. |
