# FE Step A — `_mrmr_fe_step` core/pool cluster audit (2026-07-22)

## Scope

`src/mlframe/feature_selection/filters/_mrmr_fe_step/{__init__.py, _helpers.py, _step_core.py, _step_pool.py}` — the
FE-step orchestrator (`_run_fe_step` / `_run_fe_step_impl`) and its operand-pool-construction stage. `_step_core.py`
is the ~900-line irreducible glue function that: (1) resolves the "auto"-prevalence Miller-Madow-debias knobs and
the per-fit shared FE subsample, (2) short-circuits into the (still-unimplemented, Phase-0-stub) GPU-strict-resident
FE path, (3) delegates operand-pool construction to `_step_pool.build_fe_operand_pool` (synergy bootstrap, gate-operand
reclassification, surrogate-GBM/gradient-interaction seeders), (4) delegates pair-MI computation/maxT-flooring and
pair ranking to sibling modules `_step_pairmi.py`/`_step_pairs_rank.py` (out of this cluster's scope), (5) runs the
optional smart-polynom (orthogonal-basis pair) search, (6) dispatches the expensive per-pair unary/binary operator
search either serially (GPU-active / narrow-pool) or via joblib threading, merging each chunk's prewarp/gate-med/
rejection-ledger side-channel payloads, and (7) delegates candidate materialisation/promotion and cluster-aggregate
emission to further siblings before a best-effort GPU memory-pool teardown. `_step_pool.py` builds the FE operand
pool from `selected_vars` plus the synergy/GBM/gradient seeders and carries ~120 lines of dated bench-rejection
rationale for two never-built proposers (#9 RFF pre-screen, #10 Apriori lattice). `_helpers.py`/`__init__.py` are a
clean, appropriately tiny re-export facade (53 / 21 LOC) with no issues found.

Confirmed via `mypy --cache-dir=.mlframe_mypy_cache_shared` (clean, 0 errors on all 4 files) and grep: no
eval/exec/subprocess/os.system/pickle.load/yaml.load anywhere in this cluster, and no DB/HTTP/UI surface — checklist
item 9 (SQL/HTTP/UI best practices) is confirmed **not applicable**; this is pure in-process computational
orchestration. No module-level mutable globals live in these 4 files themselves (the GPU-resident caches cleared by
`_free_gpu_fe_mempool` live in sibling modules, already covered by the 2026-07-20 audit's GPU cluster).

The prior 2026-07-20 16-agent audit's reports (`c1`..`c8`, `edge_cases`, `fe_expansion`, `gpu_residency`,
`test_coverage`) do not name any of this cluster's 4 files directly except one `fe_expansion.md` proposal mention of
`build_fe_operand_pool`, and the `_benchmarks/mrmr_critique_2026_07` critique's `_TRACKER.md`/`mrmr_crit_perf.md`
mention this package only in passing perf notes — so this cluster is largely unaudited territory. One prior claim
**is corrected** below (FE_STEP_A-4).

## Proposals

- **Wrap the GPU-strict-resident short-circuit (`_step_core.py:165-171`) behind a cheaper "is this actually
  implemented" check** rather than an unconditional call-then-catch-`NotImplementedError`, once `run_fe_step_gpu_strict`
  grows real phases — today the cost is negligible (microseconds/step) but the pattern of "always call, always catch"
  will not scale gracefully if a later phase's `run_fe_step_gpu_strict` starts doing real (but selectively-applicable)
  work before deciding to raise.
- **Add a tiny `_resolve_shared_fe_subsample(self, classes_y, X)` helper** in this package to de-duplicate
  FE_STEP_A-13's two call sites, and consider **hoisting the `check_prospective_fe_pairs` common kwargs into a
  dict built once** (FE_STEP_A-12) so the serial/joblib branches only override the handful of arguments that
  actually differ between them — halves the chance of an unnoticed future divergence.
- **Promote the `_should_serialize_fe_pair_check`/`_free_gpu_fe_mempool` direct-unit-test style** (behavioral,
  non-smoke, both flag-on and flag-off paths pinned) as the template other functions in this cluster should follow
  (FE_STEP_A-7) — they are the best-tested functions in this cluster and it shows.
- Given `_step_pairs_rank.py` (746 LOC) and especially `_step_score.py` (1131 LOC, already over both the repo
  guideline and its own stated ceiling by a wider margin) live in the same package and are presumably owned by a
  sibling audit cluster, flag to whichever cluster owns them that the whole `_mrmr_fe_step` package (3738 LOC across
  7 files) has grown past "carve before nearing 800-900" on 3 of its 7 files — a package-wide LOC checkpoint next
  time a new FE-step feature lands would be cheaper than another emergency carve.
