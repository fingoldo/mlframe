## Verdict
Fix first: SEC-01 (confirmed exploit of the default "safe" loader), then SEC-02 (sidecar-optional bare pickle and unrestricted legacy joblib on the main predict path), then SEC-04 (the pin flag that does nothing).
Not worth fixing: SEC-08 and the _benchmarks `pickle.load`/`exec` (local, self-written, nosec-justified); cloudpickle traffic to joblib workers; nltk/gitpython/jupyterlab/pypdf CVEs (dev-env only).
Structural note: allowlist/denylist unpicklers cannot make untrusted pickle safe; the durable fix is an HMAC/signature over bundles with a key held by the loader, not more list entries.
