## Checked and clean (do not re-audit)
- Bounded module caches: `_BASELINE_CV_MEMO`(64), `_Y_DENSE_MEMO`(8, 1-D y only), `_SHIFTED_Y_CACHE`, `_INFONET_Y_PREP_CACHE`, `_YZ_CARD_CACHE`(128), `_cb_border_cache` (bounded folds, tempfiles removed in `finally`).
- `_COMBO_IDX_CACHE`, `_BIN_BOUNDARIES_CACHE`, `_QLEVELS_CACHE`: unbounded keys but tiny int vectors.
- `composite/discovery/__init__.py:168-201` drops `_df_ref` in `__getstate__` and after fit: correct. `preprocessing/cleaning.py:134-163` has a byte guard before `df.copy()`.
- OOM handling in the FE GPU paths is thorough (MemoryError / cupy OutOfMemoryError with CPU fallback, `fe_gpu_has_vram_cushion`, `_feature_engineering_mem_budget.py`); WinError 1455 fan-out addressed at `_mrmr_class_config.py:329-335`; `calibration/policy.py` and `variance_gated_pairwise_diff.py` pre-check projected size.
- `_pairs_core.py:1235` shuts its pipeline executor down; `_step_pairmi.py:438` uses `shutdown(wait=False)` by design; `_fit_constant_key` hashes through a memoryview with no copy; `to_pandas()` in the suite (`_pipeline_fit_transform.py:247`) is only on the explicit `fallback_to_sklearn` branch.

## Verdict and what is NOT worth fixing
No default-path unbounded whole-frame copy was found in the training suite itself, so zero P1. Real exposures: (a) GPU resident tables and the F-order/memmap caches that outlive their fit (MEM-01..03: 4GB-card and disk-fill risks); (b) the deep `X.copy()` on "nothing engineered" in 26 FE entry points (MEM-05, cheapest large win: `deep=False`); (c) BorutaShap's multi-copy working set (MEM-04, already a recorded TODO). Not worth fixing: the tiny device-vector caches, the 1-D `y_dense.copy()`, benchmark-only `pd.concat` loops, the ranking gather for genuinely unsorted groups (inherent), MEM-11 beyond hygiene. Counts: P1 0, P2 7, P3 5.
