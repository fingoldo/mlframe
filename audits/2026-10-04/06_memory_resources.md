## Verdict and what is NOT worth fixing
No default-path unbounded whole-frame copy was found in the training suite itself, so zero P1. Real exposures: (a) GPU resident tables and the F-order/memmap caches that outlive their fit (MEM-01..03: 4GB-card and disk-fill risks); (b) the deep `X.copy()` on "nothing engineered" in 26 FE entry points (MEM-05, cheapest large win: `deep=False`); (c) BorutaShap's multi-copy working set (MEM-04, already a recorded TODO). Not worth fixing: the tiny device-vector caches, the 1-D `y_dense.copy()`, benchmark-only `pd.concat` loops, the ranking gather for genuinely unsorted groups (inherent), MEM-11 beyond hygiene. Counts: P1 0, P2 7, P3 5.
