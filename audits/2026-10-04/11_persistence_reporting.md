## Checked and found consistent (no finding)
- training/io.py `atomic_write_bytes`: same-dir tmp, unique name, os.replace, fd-adoption handling; `.meta.json` sidecar utc-aware, sort_keys, utf-8. `load_mlframe_model` cache key (abspath, mtime_ns, safe) invalidates on overwrite.
- ECE: policy._ece_score, metrics/calibration compute_ece_and_brier_decomposition (same fixed equal-width [0,1] grid) and reporting standard_ece are mutually consistent; docstrings state equal-mass ECE (calibration/quality) is not comparable. No cross-module mismatch.
- Other orjson/json hash sites (spec.py, _protocol_types.py, _recipe_unary_binary.py, _meta_fe_recommender.py, fingerprint.py) use sort_keys. np.save in _key_bank uses explicit `.npy` names (no staging-suffix bug).
- Figure close hygiene OK in reporting/* renderers, importance.py, calibration plots, boruta _io_plot; composite/diagnostics.py and pdp_2d.py return figures and delegate closing (call sites UNVERIFIED).

## Verdict
No P1 in the default model save/load path (bundle writes atomic, sidecar-gated). Counts: P1 0, P2 4 (F1-F4), P3 9 (F5-F13). Not worth fixing: ~60 `_benchmarks/` writers, F12 (ASCII payloads), F11 (best-effort cache), F9 beyond a docstring fix.
