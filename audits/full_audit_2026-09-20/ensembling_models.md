# Full audit 2026-09-20 - model zoo, ensembling and model selection

Scope: `src/mlframe/models/` (ensembling package, selection, tuning entry points), `src/mlframe/votenrank/`, `src/mlframe/estimators/`, `src/mlframe/competition/`, `src/mlframe/training/strategies/` and the ensemble wiring in `src/mlframe/training/core/` (weight fit, member gates, blending, threshold tuning, predict-time replay). `src/mlframe/training/composite/` is excluded (owned by another audit).

The package is unusually well documented about its own leakage surfaces: the gate deliberately prefers OOF/calib over val, `compare_ensembles` warns on `val.*`/`test.*` sort metrics, and `_choose_ensemble_flavour` warns when it falls back to `test.*`. The findings below are therefore mostly not "someone selected on test" but the quieter class the brief asked for: a weight fit that never runs, weights and survivor sets that are computed at train time and then thrown away before predict, a NaN member that walks straight through the gate, and a decision threshold tuned on one arbitrary member's probabilities and then applied to the blend. All of these leave a working, non-crashing, silently worse ensemble.
