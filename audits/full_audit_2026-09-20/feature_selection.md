## Checked and cleared

- **Parallelism**: every `joblib.Parallel` site in scope uses the threading backend with per-task seeds and no shared mutable state. `boruta_shap/_fit_explain.py:185-221` clones the model and varies both the row subsample and the estimator `random_state` per sub-fit; `filters/stability.py:206` maps over explicit `seeds`. No finding.
- `filters/discretization/` - the public surface (`discretize_array`, `discretize_2d_quantile_batch`, `categorize_1d_array`, `_native_kbins_quantile`) is stateless per call; no fit/transform object carries edges across folds.
- `filters/mrmr/_mrmr_class.py`, `filters/_mrmr_validate_transform.py` - MRMR is a pure filter with no internal train/val split; `transform()` replays frozen recipes and raises on column drift rather than re-deriving a quantiser.
- `filters/_screen_predictors.py` - the shared subsample covers both the relevance sweep and its maxT floor; the maxT cache key includes `int(factors_data.shape[0])` and is fit-scoped (`_mrmr_fit_impl/_fit_impl_core.py:1731`).
- `shap_proxied_fs/_shap_proxy_explain.py::compute_shap_matrix` - the OOF path is honest: `_one_fold` fits on `X.iloc[tr_idx]` and attributes only `X.iloc[va_idx]`.
- `filters/_grouped_quantile_fe.py::generate_target_aware_group_bins` - per-fold MDLP edges, including the small-group fallback, are refit on train-only rows; all-rows edges are used only for the replay recipe.
