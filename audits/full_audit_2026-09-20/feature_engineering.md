# Full audit 2026-09-20 - Feature engineering

Read in full or in the relevant part: `src/mlframe/feature_engineering/` - `basic.py` (date/cyclical features), `numerical.py` (numaggs, rolling MA, entropy/distributional aggregates), `grouped_rank.py` (`per_group_rank` kernels incl. causal/tiebreak variants), `row_wise_extremality.py` + `row_wise_extremality_reference.py`, `entity_inter_event.py`, `recency_aggregation.py`, `recency_weighted_rolling.py`, `multi_window_aggregate.py`, `bruteforce.py`, `cat_cooccurrence_svd.py`, `holiday_locale_target_encoding.py`, `rolling_target_correlation.py`, `graph_features.py`, `transformer/_oof.py`, `transformer/_suite_adapter.py`; and `src/mlframe/preprocessing/` - `transforms.py`, `auto_transform_select.py`, `unseen_category_imputer.py`, `cleaning.py` (scan), `adversarial_rebin.py`. Cross-checked the two production consumers of the row-wise FE block, `training/pipeline/_pipeline_extensions.py` and `training/core/_predict_pre_pipeline.py`, plus `training/core/_phase_helpers_fit_pipeline.py` (replay-config construction). Several suspicions were tested and dropped: `median_fill_polars`'s selector-to-selector `fill_nan` expands positionally per column (verified on polars 1.44.1, each column gets its OWN median); polars `rename` tolerates keys absent from the frame, so `run_pysr_fe`'s over-wide `rename_map` does not raise; `rolling_target_correlation_tracker` shifts BOTH `X[c]` and `y` by 1 and is genuinely strictly-past; `holiday_name_target_encode_cross_locale`'s running sums are `cumsum() - y` (self-excluding) and causal.
