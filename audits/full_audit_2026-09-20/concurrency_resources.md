# Full audit 2026-09-20 - concurrency, resource management and process-level state

Scope: the whole `src/mlframe` package except `training/composite/`, read-only. The package is already defensive about most of this class of bug: nearly every module-level cache in `feature_selection/filters` carries its own `threading.Lock`, per-fit knob overrides were migrated off `os.environ` onto thread-locals (`_fourier_detect_cap.py`), pyplot is avoided in the renderers in favour of bare `Figure` + `FigureCanvasAgg`, and both daemon watchers (`Heartbeat`, `CatBoostGpuFitMonitor`) use monotonic clocks and refuse to self-join. What remains is concentrated in four places: one genuinely unlocked device-buffer singleton on a path that MRMR dispatches with `backend="threading"`, watchdogs whose abandoned work keeps consuming the resource the fallback then re-acquires, process-global state (`os.environ`, `torch.cuda.is_available`, root logger) mutated without restore, and reproducibility claims that do not survive crossing a thread boundary (verified experimentally for numba's RNG). Findings are ordered by severity; every LOW is listed.
